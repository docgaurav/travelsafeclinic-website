# TravelSafe Clinic Website (`travelsafeclinic.in`)

Official 2026 website for **TravelSafe Clinic**, India's authoritative international travel health, study abroad student vaccination, and pre-travel prophylaxis center led by Dr. Gaurav Gupta (ISTM & ASTMH certified).

## Features
- **Cross-Border Compass**: Outbound student university clearances (US/UK/Canada/Australia) & inbound partner travel health.
- **QuantiFERON-TB Gold Plus**: Pre-departure blood testing guidance without BCG false positives.
- **The Delhi Belly & Rabies PrEP Shield**: Food & water security and travel kits for international partners visiting India.
- **Special Situations**: Mountain treks (Everest Base Camp, Kilimanjaro, Ladakh, Diamox protocols), biologic therapies, and Hajj/Umrah pilgrimages.
- **Direct WhatsApp Consultation Dispatcher**: Sends structured triage requests directly to clinic staff (`+91 9653010203`).
- **Cloudflare Pages Optimized**: Instant global edge caching with automatic SSL.
